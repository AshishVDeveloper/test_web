📹 Connect Person

Video, voice and chat straight between devices. No sign-in.

📞

Private call (1 to 1)

Only you and one other person. You let them in; after that the call is locked and nobody else can join, even with the code.

👥

Meeting (group)

Up to 12 people. Everyone sees, hears and chats with everyone. You let each person in and can remove people.

Join with a code

Got a code for a call or a meeting? Enter it here, or just open the link or scan the QR code you were given.

When you start, people must also type this password. Tell it to them separately, not in the same message as the link.

Your browser will ask to use the camera and microphone. Without them you can still watch, listen and chat. Nothing is recorded or saved here, but no website can stop someone from recording their own screen.
